# Cost baseline per reply kind, 2026-10-26

Generated by `python3 -I tools/cost-baseline/baseline.py` from 32 Missions with stream dumps or a cost log. Prices are the published per-MTok rates in `PRICES` in the script; each cache write is priced at the TTL the API reported for it. Cost is summed over every `usage.iterations` entry, so compaction is included.

Answers #244. Everything below "All Missions" is the script's output. These findings were written by hand from it.

## Findings

- **Spend.** $18.09 over 32 Missions: $17.10 in 232 priced responses, plus $0.98 in Craft Checks and margin rechecks.
  Sequence Plans take 54%, and next-leg Sequence Plans alone take $8.78. Debriefs take 18% and revised Mission Plans 11%.
- **Output and thinking.** Output is 52% of the dumped spend. Thinking is 76% of output tokens, which is 40% of spend.
- **Cache.** Cache writes are 43.5% of spend and reads are 4.2%. Uncached input is nil.
- **Cut-offs.** There are 3, all in the Mun Mission, costing $1.49 (8.7%). Each one ran a compaction and then spent the
  whole 32,000-token budget on thinking, with no text at all. The two retries ran the compaction again, because a
  compaction that is never committed is lost.
- **Not committed.** 7 responses ($1.93, 11.3%) never reached the transcript: the 3 cut-offs, 1 off-schema Debrief, and
  3 more from the older Missions.
- **Cancelled streams.** 8 streams were cancelled (`kept cancelled`) or were still open when the game quit. They have no
  final usage, so they are not priced.
- **Mid-Mission cache misses.** By the stated test (request 3 or later, no compaction, write > read) there are **19**.
  13 of them are request 3 or 4 of the 24 Oct Missions, where the first Debrief or Script Check rewrite reads back only
  the system prefix. None of these appear after 24 Oct. The other 6 are all revised Mission Plans (after a manual
  stretch ×2, after an Off-Nominal Debrief ×2, a player change, a reduced goal), and their read falls back to the
  13–31K system prefix. Re-writing a prefix that the previous request had already cached cost $0.76 over the read
  price (4.4%).
- **5-minute writes.** 11,802 tokens were written with the 5-minute TTL, not 1h. They are the message iterations of
  the 4 compaction responses: right after a compaction, the breakpoint is written at 5m.
- **The mod's price table over-reads.** `PriceTable.CacheReadMultiplier` is 0.1, but Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 cache hits
  cost 0.05× input. On the 160 responses with a `paid` line, the mod charged $14.64 against a published-rate cost of
  $13.98, about 4.7% high. Re-pricing at 0.1× reproduces the mod's figure exactly.
- **Failed legs.** There are 77 Sequence Plans for 58 distinct legs. Of 59 Debriefs, 28 are Off-Nominal and 9 Failed.
- **Mun Mission `m20261026-031725-724a`.** $5.90 in all: $5.52 dumped plus $0.38 in 12 Craft Checks. It made 35
  requests, reached a 147,520-token max context and compacted 4 times. It flew 12 Sequence Plans for 7 legs, and 6 of
  its 11 Debriefs were Off-Nominal (none Failed). Compaction is 18.1% of its spend, the cut-offs are 27.1%, and its
  responses that were never committed are 30.6%.
